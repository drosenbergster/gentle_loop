## What Was Done This Session

### 1. System Prompt v3.0 (Knowledge Enrichment)

`supabase/functions/ai-suggest/system-prompt.ts` was upgraded from v2.1 to v3.0 with 20+ new knowledge areas:

- **Patchwork abilities** — a person can know lettuce gets soggy but can't make the sandwich
- **Object agnosia** — can't recognize objects visually; may recognize by touch
- **Face non-recognition** — may still recognize voice, touch, or smell
- **Sensory sensitivity** — clothing/fabric can become intolerable; detailed alternatives
- **Caregiver stress contagion** — emotional tone is contagious
- **Hidden medical causes** — UTIs, infections, dehydration masquerade as worsening dementia
- **Identity/role preservation** — repetitive hosting offers are about identity, not tea
- **Perseveration / task restart loops** — environmental completion signals break loops
- **Bedtime loops** — environmental cues, avoid reasoning
- **Medication refusal** — address the interaction, not the medication
- **Ambiguous loss** — grieving someone still alive, no closure
- **Respite guilt** — permission + practical resources
- **Caregiver isolation** — validate, don't fix
- **Kitchen/appliance safety** — removable stove knobs, auto shutoff, microwave
- **Stairs safety** — contrast tape, handrails, patterned carpet danger
- **Visitor management** — "don't quiz, announce who you are"
- **PAINAD pain recognition** — vocal sounds, rigid body, clenched fists
- **Assistive technology** — GPS trackers, pill dispensers, sensors, monitors
- **DICE framework** — implicit reasoning in core philosophy
- **Bathing alternatives ladder** — full bath down to no-rinse products
- **Watch-me and hand-over-hand techniques** (Teepa Snow)
- **911 dementia disclosure** — tell responders the person has dementia

Sources: alz.org daily care plans, Teepa Snow Positive Approach to Care, DICE framework, NIA, Validation Method (Naomi Feil), Montessori-based dementia care, Music & Memory, PAINAD, and real caregiver scenarios provided by user.

### 2. Model Upgrade (Required)

### 3. Stress-Test Suite (38 Scenarios)

`app/scripts/prompt-validation.ts` extended from 14 to 38 scenarios:

- **2 crisis edge cases** (CR-1, CR-2) — "I almost hit her", exhaustion vs. despair

## Known Issues

- **Anthropic rate limiting**: Test suite needs 8s between requests + retry logic to avoid 429s. This is an API-tier issue, not an app issue.
- **Brevity**: Model sometimes ignores the 40-word limit on complex scenarios (up to 118 words observed). Token limit acts as a hard ceiling but may truncate mid-sentence if too low.
- **`_bmad/` framework**: The BMAD framework installer has ~120 modified/new files that are NOT part of our work. These are excluded from commits.
